# DroneLink support

DroneLink shows your DJI drone's live camera on your Windows PC, then puts it in OBS, on phones on the same Wi-Fi, in an MP4 recording, or live on YouTube and Facebook.

- **Get DroneLink:** Microsoft Store (search "DroneLink")
- **Privacy policy:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)
- **Help or a bug report:** [open an issue](https://github.com/hksit/dronelink-support/issues/new) and describe what you see, your drone model and app (DJI Fly, DJI GO 4 or DJI Pilot 2), and your Windows version.

## Quick start

1. Join the computer and the phone or controller running DJI Fly to the **same Wi-Fi**.
2. Open DroneLink and click **Copy address** (or scan the QR code).
3. In DJI Fly tap **···** → **Transmission** → **Live Streaming Platforms** → **RTMP**, paste the address and tap **Start Live**.
4. The picture appears in DroneLink. Use the tabs to show it in OBS, let others watch, record or go live.

No drone handy? Click **Fly a test drone** → **Take off** to try everything with a simulated flight.

## Common fixes

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| DJI Fly cannot connect | Put the phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi, turn off mobile data on the phone, and allow DroneLink when Windows asks about network access. |
| Works at home but not at a venue | Guest Wi-Fi often blocks devices from talking to each other. Turn on the phone's hotspot and join the computer to it. |
| Picture stops when the pilot checks messages | Keep DJI Fly open on screen while streaming. |
| OBS shows nothing | Add a **Browser** source with the address from DroneLink's **Show in OBS** tab. |
